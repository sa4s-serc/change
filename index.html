Our Position

Moving Beyond static MLOps

Traditional MLOps assumes behavior can be governed through versioning and monitoring. This fails once agents learn after release. CHANGE provides six essential capabilities to manage systems that think and evolve.

Contextualize

Encodes an agent’s learning trajectory, enabling stable adaptation by replaying operational history.

"Alice's unique experiential context is managed as a versioned artifact, moving beyond model versions."

Harmonize

Maintains alignment in multi-agent settings via behavioral consensus protocols and shared knowledge spaces.

"Alice and Bob align on shipping policies by comparing summarized reasoning traces in shared memory."

Anticipate

Forecasts behavioral drift by mirroring agents in a digital-twin sandbox to explore outcomes before production.

"Alice’s simulated counterpart reacts to challenging refund cases in a sandbox, allowing for early intervention."

Negotiate

Dynamically manages autonomy and oversight. Agents request permission for cases outside policy boundaries.

"Alice requests temporary permission to handle a unique refund direttamente, providing her reasoning to a supervisor."

Generate

Enables systems to grow by allowing agents to propose, test, and integrate new tools based on experience.

"Alice identifies a bottleneck and proposes an automated tool to consolidate carrier shipment data."

Evolve

Governs long-term adaptation through Knowledge Distillation, transferring nuance to new successors.

"Alice's strategies are distilled into Alex, her successor, ensuring knowledge is preserved as patterns align."
