Our Position

Moving Beyond static MLOps

Traditional MLOps assumes behavior can be governed through versioning and monitoring. This fails once agents learn after release. CHANGE provides six essential capabilities to manage systems that think and evolve.

Contextualize

Encodes an agent’s learning trajectory, enabling stable adaptation by replaying operational history as a versioned artifact.

"Alice's interactions create a unique experiential context, treated as an evolving history rather than a model version."

Harmonize

Maintains alignment via behavioral consensus protocols and shared spaces where agents align on goals.

"Alice and Bob detect conflicting recommendations in shared logistics and trigger a consensus step to handle returns."

Anticipate

Forecasts behavioral drift by mirroring agents in a digital-twin sandbox to explore novel scenarios.

"The system identifies early signs of Alice's behavioral drift and tests her simulated counterpart in a controlled sandbox."

Negotiate

Dynamically manages autonomy and oversight. Agents request permission for cases outside policy boundaries.

"Alice requests temporary permission to handle a complex refund directly, providing an explanation of her reasoning to a supervisor."

Generate

Enables systems to grow by allowing agents to propose, test, and integrate new tools based on experience.

"Alice notices a latency bottleneck and proposes an automated query tool that consolidates carrier data into the system."

Evolve

Governs long-term adaptation through Knowledge Distillation, transferring nuance to new successors.

"Alice's strategies are distilled into a training corpus for her successor, Alex, ensuring knowledge is preserved while patterns align."
